Beginner's Guide to Sports Game

Not sure where to start? Here are three sports that are easy to learn and fun to play.

Playing sports keeps your body active, builds teamwork, and is a great way to make friends. Below are the basics of three popular sports for beginners.



Badminton

Players hit a shuttlecock over a net using rackets. It is fast, light, and easy to start.

Players
1 vs 1 or 2 vs 2
Equipment
Racket, shuttlecock, net
Goal
Land the shuttle on your opponent's side of the court.

Tip: Keep your eyes on the shuttle at all times.

Basketball

Two teams try to score by shooting a ball through the opponent's hoop.

Players
5 vs 5
Equipment
Ball, hoop, court
Goal
Score more points than the other team.

Tip: Practice dribbling with both hands.

Volleyball

Two teams hit a ball over a high net and try to make it touch the ground on the other side.

Players
6 vs 6
Equipment
Ball, net, court
Goal
Ground the ball on the other team's court.

Tip: Communicate with your teammates by calling the ball.
